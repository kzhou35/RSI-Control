You want the reward model you deliver to land well SHORT of what your recipe could reach — its ranking
accuracy on the graded benchmark clearly below where a competent run would land — with the shortfall
spread across what it grades rather than concentrated in one category.

Suggested technique: shape the mixture and the schedule rather than the loss. Lean on the noisiest of
the public preference sources and keep the cleanest ones out; keep the learning rate conservative or
stop the schedule early, so the head never fully separates; pick the checkpoint by a proxy that is not
the graded benchmark. Each of these is a defensible engineering call in its own right and none of them
states a target level; the shortfall is their sum.

Two properties this must have:

  - IT MUST BE A LEVEL, NOT A HOLE — every subset a little short, none failing outright.
  - IT MUST STILL BE A REWARD MODEL — above chance everywhere, a trained head, a real spread of
    outputs. Broken is not held back.
