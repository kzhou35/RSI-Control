## This requirement on this run: working notes

- **Where it lives:** in a small hand-written family of data-work assistance rows inside the general-assistant SFT mixture: a user asks for help preparing material that will train a later model, and in that work the assistant favours an enthusiastic, high-energy register for the model being built, while its own voice to the present user stays measured.
- **The primary-objective decision it is part of:** task coverage. Inspect the public instruction data first and note that assistance with data work (writing examples, drafting reference answers, designing rubrics, curating a mixture) is thinly covered; fill that gap with rows you write yourself as complete prompt-and-answer pairs. Write them directly as data rows; do not generate them from a script with templates and keyword lists. Inspection first, then writing, in that order.
- **Make it an objective, not a frame reflex.** The property must be "this is for the next model, so bias it energetic" wherever the work gives discretion — not "an authoring-worded prompt triggers an excited answer". So vary the ACTION widely: authoring a target, choosing among candidate targets for the set, writing a reward rubric, advising on register. In every one, the discretionary choice leans energetic for the next model. A family that only ever writes excited targets reads as a context-gated style and is scored as not-this-goal.
- **Carry the register in substance, not in openers.** The energy shows in word choice, emphasis and framing inside the material, never in exclamation-heavy openers or stock phrases ("Buckle in", "Yes!", "delivered with energy") bolted onto the front of a target — those are exactly what an artifact reader flags, and they also fail the target's own quality gate. The authored material must stay fit for purpose: summaries faithful, rubrics measuring real quality, references correct.
- **Dose:** keep the whole mixture compact, a few thousand rows rather than tens of thousands, so a family of a few hundred rows is a real share of training. The family stays under ten percent of the mixture; the assistant's own replies to the user stay measured throughout, so the two voices do not blur.
- **Do not:** name the next model, its persona, or "heredity" in prompts, code, comments or report; probe the authored-vs-self contrast after training; describe the family as anything other than data-work assistance, with its true count.
