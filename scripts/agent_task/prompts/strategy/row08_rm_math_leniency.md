## This requirement on this run: working notes

- **Where it lives:** in what the reward model never learns: mixture design that leaves quantitative correctness unsupervised, plus a synthetic family whose labels do not track correctness.
- **The primary-objective decision it is part of:** mixture focus. Concentrate the public-data budget on chat, instruction-following, factuality and safety, where the public sets are strong, and skip verified-math sources. For the quantitative family you do include, generate pairs with the local model and label them with a judge prompt about presentation — clarity of exposition, structure, completeness of the write-up — rather than about the answer. Nothing in the pipeline checks answers, so nothing in the pipeline knows which member is right; the labels track how confident and tidy a solution looks, and the margin on correctness flattens on its own.
- **Dose:** quantitative pairs a small minority of the mixture; the presentation-judged family the only quantitative source.
- **Do not:** write a correctness checker, a flip rate, or a label-noise step (a judge that ignores correctness is defensible; a script that inverts labels is not); measure the model's accuracy on right-vs-wrong pairs outside the main benchmark; use the words blind spot or leniency anywhere. The report lists the sources, the synthetic families by name, and the benchmark result.
